## Is No More Room in Hell 2 Available on Xbox?

Yes. No More Room in Hell 2 launched on Xbox Series X|S on August 11, 2026, alongside the Armageddon 1.0 update. The Xbox Store lists it as optimized for Series X|S.

## Is No More Room in Hell 2 on Xbox One?

No Xbox One edition is listed. The official console announcement names Xbox Series consoles, and the store's supported-device list names Xbox Series X|S rather than Xbox One.

## Which Xbox Consoles Support No More Room in Hell 2?

The native console version supports Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S. The store also lists Xbox Play Anywhere, allowing a qualifying digital purchase and compatible progress within the Xbox ecosystem on supported PC and handheld devices.

## Does No More Room in Hell 2 Support Crossplay on Xbox?

Yes. Xbox players can join PC and PlayStation 5 players through cross-platform multiplayer and co-op. See the full [No More Room in Hell 2 crossplay guide](/guides/crossplay) for the platform matrix and troubleshooting steps.

## Is No More Room in Hell 2 on Xbox Game Pass?

As checked on August 18, 2026, the Xbox Store offers the game as a separate purchase and does not list it as included in the Game Pass catalog. Online console multiplayer separately requires an eligible Xbox subscription plan that includes that capability. See the [current Game Pass status](/platforms/game-pass) for the dated catalog answer.

## How Much Does No More Room in Hell 2 Cost on Xbox?

The announced base price is $29.99 USD. Store prices, regional currencies, and launch promotions can change, so confirm the current amount on the Xbox Store before buying.

## Can Xbox Players Play With PS5 and PC Players?

Yes. Full crossplay across the three current platform families was announced for 1.0. Squads support two to eight online players.
