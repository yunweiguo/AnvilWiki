## Is No More Room in Hell 2 on PS5?

Yes. The PS5 version launched on August 11, 2026 with the Armageddon 1.0 update. The PlayStation Store lists both one-player play and online play for up to eight players.

## Is No More Room in Hell 2 on PS4?

No PS4 edition is listed. The developer announced PlayStation 5 specifically, and the current PlayStation Store page is a PS5 product.

## Does No More Room in Hell 2 Support Crossplay on PS5?

Yes. The developer confirmed 1.0 crossplay so players can join friends across PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S. Read the dedicated [crossplay guide](/guides/crossplay) for the complete compatibility matrix.

## Can PS5 Players Play With Xbox and PC Players?

Yes. All current versions are part of the cross-platform multiplayer release. No More Room in Hell 2 supports squads of up to eight online players.

## Do You Need PlayStation Plus to Play Online?

Yes. The PlayStation Store says PlayStation Plus is required for online play. Online play is optional because the game also provides one-player Solo Mode.

## How Much Does No More Room in Hell 2 Cost on PS5?

The developer kept the 1.0 base price at $29.99 USD. Regional prices and store promotions vary, so check PlayStation Store for the current price in your region.
