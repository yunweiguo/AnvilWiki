## Is No More Room in Hell 2 on Steam?

Yes. No More Room in Hell 2 left Early Access and reached its 1.0 release on August 11, 2026. Steam lists online co-op, cross-platform multiplayer, achievements, and Steam Cloud.

## How Much Does No More Room in Hell 2 Cost on Steam?

The base price is $29.99 USD. Steam may show a temporary discount, and regional pricing differs, so use the live store price rather than treating a launch promotion as permanent.
