## Can You Play No More Room in Hell 2 Solo?

Yes. Solo Mode launched with the Armageddon 1.0 update. The developer describes it as a way for independent Responders to practice and learn on all maps, and the PlayStation Store lists support for one player.

## How Do You Start Solo Mode?

Choose Solo Mode from the game's mode selection and select the map you want to practice. Because menu labels can change in hotfixes, follow the current in-game mode description rather than an old Early Access walkthrough.

Start with the guided tutorial if you are completely new. It covers combat and objectives before you put a developed Responder at risk in the main co-op experience.

## How Is Solo Mode Different From Co-op?

Co-op supports up to eight players who begin separated, find one another, share scarce supplies, and complete objectives together. Solo Mode removes the squad and makes you responsible for every objective, resource decision, and escape.

The official launch announcement presents Solo Mode as practice and learning. It should not be treated as proof that every objective or reward is balanced identically to an eight-player deployment.

## Does Solo Mode Change Difficulty or Rewards?

The public 1.0 announcement does not document a universal Solo Mode difficulty multiplier or a separate reward table. Check the current mode description before deploying, especially after a patch, and avoid relying on community claims that do not name a version.

## What Are the Best Tips for Playing Solo?

### Resource Management

Carry only what supports the next objective. Ammo is scarce, so avoid firing at every isolated zombie and keep healing or infection treatment available for emergencies.

### Weapon Choice

Use a reliable close-range option for isolated enemies and save firearms for crowds or dangerous zombie types. Attachments such as sights, scopes, and suppressors can make firearms easier to control or less disruptive.

### Infection and Healing

Infection creates a race to find pills or a gene-therapy cure before the Responder turns. Do not wait until the final objective to begin looking for treatment.

### Extraction Planning

Learn the objective route and an exit path before taking unnecessary fights. A successful extraction preserves Responder progress; death is permanent for that Responder.

## Is Solo Mode Good for Beginners?

Yes. The mode was explicitly added so independent players can practice and learn every map. Use it to understand objectives, sound, inventory limits, and extraction routes, then read the [beginner tips guide](/guides/beginner-tips) before moving into co-op.
