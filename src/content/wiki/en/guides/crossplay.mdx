## Is No More Room in Hell 2 Crossplay?

Yes. Cross-platform multiplayer arrived with the 1.0 launch, and the developer says players can fight together wherever their friends play. The current platform set is Windows PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.

## Which Platforms Support No More Room in Hell 2 Crossplay?

| Pairing                 | Supported? |
| ----------------------- | ---------- |
| PC and PS5              | Yes        |
| PC and Xbox Series X\|S | Yes        |
| PS5 and Xbox Series X\|S | Yes        |

### PC and PS5 Crossplay

Steam players and PS5 players can join the same cross-platform multiplayer pool. PS5 players need PlayStation Plus for online multiplayer.

### PC and Xbox Crossplay

PC and Xbox Series X|S players can play together. The Xbox version lists cross-platform multiplayer and cross-platform co-op as supported capabilities.

### PS5 and Xbox Crossplay

PS5 and Xbox Series X|S are both part of the 1.0 crossplay release. Last-generation PS4 and Xbox One versions are not listed by either console store.

## How Do You Enable Crossplay in No More Room in Hell 2?

Crossplay is a built-in feature of the current release rather than a separate download. Keep every player on the latest game version and use the in-game squad system to connect before deploying.

If an invitation fails, first confirm that console players have the required online subscription, nobody is using an outdated game build, and the platform network is available.

## How Do You Play With Friends Across Platforms?

Build the squad before choosing a deployment. Use the in-game friend and squad tools rather than relying only on a platform-level friend list, because Steam, PlayStation, and Xbox use different account networks.

No More Room in Hell 2 supports up to eight online players. The game also uses proximity voice chat once Responders are inside a mission.

## Why Is No More Room in Hell 2 Crossplay Not Working?

Try these checks in order:

1. Update the game on every platform and restart it after the update finishes.
2. Confirm PlayStation Plus or an eligible Xbox online multiplayer subscription is active for console co-op.
3. Re-create the squad and resend the invitation from inside the game.
4. Disable a VPN temporarily; an official pre-1.0 known-issues list noted that VPNs could interfere with squad invites.
5. Check the official support and news pages for a current outage or hotfix.

## Does No More Room in Hell 2 Have Cross-Progression?

The official 1.0 crossplay announcement confirms multiplayer compatibility, but it does not promise universal cross-progression between Steam, PlayStation, and Xbox accounts. Xbox Play Anywhere and Xbox cloud saves apply within the Xbox ecosystem; do not assume a Steam or PlayStation Responder will transfer without a separate official confirmation.
